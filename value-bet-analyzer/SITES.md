# Multi-Site Stats Scraper Guide

## 🌐 Supported Websites

The scraper now works with **4 different stats sites**! Use whichever you prefer:

### 1. **FBref.com** ⭐ (Recommended)
- ✅ Most detailed statistics
- ✅ Easy to navigate
- ✅ Reliable data structure
- 📊 Best for: Complete team analysis

### 2. **Sofascore.com**
- ✅ Clean, modern interface
- ✅ Mobile-friendly
- ✅ Live scores + stats
- 📊 Best for: Quick lookups

### 3. **FlashScore.com**
- ✅ Fast loading
- ✅ Excellent H2H data
- ✅ Global coverage
- 📊 Best for: International teams

### 4. **WhoScored.com**
- ✅ Advanced analytics
- ✅ Player ratings
- ✅ Tactical insights
- 📊 Best for: Deep analysis

## 🚀 How to Use

### Step 1: Run the Scraper
Double-click **`collect_stats.bat`** in `Documents/BettingBot`

### Step 2: Navigate to ANY Supported Site
The browser opens to Google. Search for:
- "Arsenal FBref"
- "Barcelona Sofascore"
- "Real Madrid FlashScore"
- "Manchester United WhoScored"

### Step 3: Click Team Page
Navigate to the team's page on any of the 4 sites

### Step 4: Auto-Scrape! ✨
The scraper **automatically detects** which site you're on and extracts stats

### Step 5: Repeat for Other Teams
- Use different sites for different teams
- Mix and match as you prefer
- All data saves to the same CSV

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Use FBref for most teams** - Most reliable
2. **Use Sofascore for quick checks** - Fastest navigation
3. **Use FlashScore for H2H** - Best head-to-head data
4. **Mix sites freely** - Scraper handles all formats

## 📊 What Gets Scraped

From **all sites**:
- ✅ Last 5 match results (W/D/L)
- ✅ Goals scored (total)
- ✅ Goals conceded (total)
- ✅ Team name

## 🎯 Example Workflow

**Analyzing Mexico vs South Africa:**

1. Run `collect_stats.bat`
